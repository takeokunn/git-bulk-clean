# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project aims to provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment for
everyone participating in its issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces. Be respectful, discuss technical decisions in good faith, and
focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful to
the project or its community.

## Reporting

No private conduct-reporting contact is currently published. Do not send
conduct reports through the security-vulnerability channel unless the report
also concerns a security vulnerability. If a matter can be raised safely,
contact the maintainers through the repository's normal GitHub facilities
without publishing sensitive personal information. If no safe channel is
available, refrain from public disclosure until one is established.
